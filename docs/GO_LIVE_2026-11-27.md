## What's ready

**Conversation** (`receptionist/brain.py`). Fixes come from 8 rounds of simulated callers; there are about 1040 tests.

- **Owner facts:**
  - 50% deposit, no payment plans
  - the client owns the domain; the code can be bought separately
  - no contract, cancel anytime; the site goes off at month end; no refunds
  - 24 hours to a month to build
  - builds software and hardware
  - no portfolio
  - the receptionist is for sale, with flexible pricing
- **Sells herself.** "Can you answer my phone?" gets "You're talking to one right now…". Price and setup questions are then answered for the receptionist, not the website.
- **Never loses a lead:**
  - A name and number said in one breath are kept.
  - A number said anywhere starts a message.
  - A heard lead is saved at goodbye.
  - A short number is asked for again.
- **No unasked-for bookings.** She only offers times when the caller asks to book. "Bye" is not consent.
- **Both questions answered** when a caller asks two at once. ASR repeat loops are collapsed.
- **Kind and honest:**
  - feelings come first
  - money worries get the budget answer
  - a caller who was scammed before gets "no commitment"
  - "slower" means shorter
  - an unknown question gets an honest "I don't want to guess"
- **Spanish:**
  - A Spanish caller is answered in Spanish for the whole call: name and number, message, price, deposit, timeline, receptionist, Square, ordering, and goodbye.
  - English-ears garble of Spanish is still detected.

**Spam and minutes**

- Robocall scripts get one polite line, then a hang-up.
- Three nonsense turns from a caller who never said anything real get a kind hang-up.
- A call over 15 minutes is wrapped up (`MAX_CALL_S`, default 900).
- A line silent for 30 seconds is closed (`IDLE_HANGUP_S`, default 30).

**Lead alerts** (`receptionist/notify.py`)

- Each saved message POSTs `{name, callback, urgent, language}` to `NOTIFY_URL`. The caller's words are never sent.
- This is off until the owner sets `NOTIFY_URL`, and `NOTIFY_TOKEN` in Doppler.

## Measured, not adopted: local LLM for off-script questions (2026-11-27)

The test ran `lab/llm/fallback_bench.py` on the Pi: llama-server, 3 threads, the business facts as the system prompt (cached), 8 off-script questions, 48 tokens max.

| Model | p50 | Max | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen2.5-0.5B Q4_K_M | 3.06 s | 5.74 s | **Invents promises**: "we'll take immediate action" if hacked, "yes" to New York, fixes WordPress |
| Qwen2.5-1.5B Q4_K_M | 4.77 s | 6.06 s | Safe but mostly "Alex can answer that on a free call" |

**Verdict:** both fail the 1.5 s gate. The 0.5B model is also unsafe, because it makes up facts. The fixed-answer layer plus checked knowledge stays. Revisit with a faster or fine-tuned model, and only behind a strict facts-only filter.

## Optional: vocabulary prompt for short turns

The daemon gets an optional 5th request field, and the adapter sends it only for clips under 2 s. Test results on the Pi, phone path:

- "A las diez" was "¡Alas pies!" or "¡Alas 10!" without the prompt, and correct with it.
- "Ten." was "10" without it, and "Ten." with it.
- Long turns don't use it, because there it added 0.3 to 0.9 s and repeat loops.

To enable it, set these in voice.env, then rebuild the daemon:

```
WD_PROMPT_EN=Phone call to a receptionist. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, ten a.m., eleven, two p.m., website, receptionist, booking, appointment, yes, thanks, bye.
WD_PROMPT_ES=Sí, por favor. A las diez. A las dos de la tarde. El lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves o viernes. Una página web. Gracias.
```
